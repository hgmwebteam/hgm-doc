Decisions already made — don’t re-open

  • Block JSON is the master; the portal renders HTML live, PDF at publish. The dashboard never holds content, only pointers.
  • Publish is immediate for every department except those with requires_approval; lead is notified with a diff; one-click revert on the page.
  • Claude is behind Create SOP and Update SOP only. There is no free-text Claude box anywhere on the dashboard (plan §6, “no general chat”).
  • SOP ID is the slug: /sop/HGM-SOP-WEB-002, tasks by fragment #1.3.
  • Light theme default; no gem yellow in document colour (the dashboard chrome follows the portal, the SOP page follows the house style).

Empty and edge states

  • First launch, one department seeded. Coverage shows WEB 3 and five zeros; Needs review and Recently updated show their empty text; My open runs is absent. The page should look intentional in this state, because this is the state Gillian sees first.
  • Empty department selected. Header “Ads — 0 SOPs”, one dashed card: “No Ads SOPs yet — anyone can create one.” with Create SOP pre-filled to Ads.
  • Draft visibility. Drafts are visible to their author, the department lead and admin; they don’t count toward coverage or appear in search for anyone else.
  • Contractor login. Undecided (see below). Design the panel so a whole department can be hidden per role without a gap in the layout.

Open questions

  • Department codes changed. The house style and the authoring skill enforce WEB, CON, OPS, FIN, SEC, CLI. The new list is ACC, WEB, ADS, CON, PLA, SEC. HGM-SOP-OPS-001 (how to produce an SOP) has no department under the new list. Options: keep OPS as a seventh, company-wide department; or move OPS-001 to a new home and re-ID it. Kyle to raise with Gillian. Nothing in this wireframe depends on the answer except the seed data.
  • Department leads for ACC, ADS, CON, PLA and SEC, and which Google Chat space each notifies.
  • Contractor access — none, read-only on some departments, or full contributor.
  • Search scope — confirm the global Shift F search is Postgres-backed so SOP results can join it, or whether it needs its own index.
